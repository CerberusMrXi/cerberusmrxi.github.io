## Core Technical Competencies

### 1. Security Research & Exploit Development
- **Vulnerability Auditing**: Deep research into web vulnerabilities (SSTI, JWT forgery, SSRF, XSS, OAuth bypass), binary reverse engineering, and Bluetooth protocol security.
- **Exploit Engineering**: Authoring custom proof-of-concept (PoC) scripts for published CVEs and security research tooling.
- **Steganography & Cryptography**: Analysis of covert communication channels, steganographic data extraction, and cryptanalysis.

### 2. Artificial Intelligence & Computer Vision
- **Object Detection & Tracking**: Real-time vision systems utilizing YOLOv8s, OpenCV, and IoU temporal tracking algorithms.
- **Intelligent Systems**: Developing automated decision-making engines and embedded AI applications.

### 3. Software Engineering & Systems Architecture
- **Backend & Tooling**: Microservices, REST/GraphQL APIs, and high-performance CLI tools built with Python, TypeScript, C, and Rust.
- **Cloud & Automation**: Containerization (Docker), CI/CD security integration, and automated infrastructure.
